# Product Context: KM100 Inventory Management System

## Organization
**KM100 Skills and Training Center, Inc.**
A premier technical-vocational training institution managing classrooms, specialized training workshops, computer laboratories, and administrative facilities.

## Purpose
A modern, responsive, high-reliability inventory tracking application designed to track equipment, tools, computers, training modules, and supplies across multiple rooms with custom schema support and institutional Excel export capabilities.

## Target Audience
- Training Center Administrators
- Laboratory Custodians and Technical Assistants
- Facility and Property Officers
- Department Heads and Instructors

## Key Value Drivers
1. **Zero-Setup Portability**: Instant launch in any modern browser without complex server requirements.
2. **Dynamic Room Organization**: Track items per room (Computer Lab, Training Rooms, Admin Office, Tool Storage) with badge summaries.
3. **Flexible Schema Builder**: Adapt to different equipment categories by creating custom columns (Text, Number, Dropdown) that dynamically update forms, table views, and Excel spreadsheets.
4. **Institutional Excel Export**: Multi-sheet workbooks with institutional headers, official formatting, and current room exports.
5. **Data Resilience**: Real-time auto-saving to `localStorage` plus portable JSON backup/restore for device migration.
